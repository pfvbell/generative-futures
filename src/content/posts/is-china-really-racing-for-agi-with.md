"China is racing us to AGI" has become one of the most powerful sentences in AI politics — used to justify speed, secrecy, and deregulation. This episode asks whether it's actually true.

Seán Ó hÉigeartaigh has spent a decade watching China's AI ecosystem up close, and his answer is: much less than you'd think. China's official strategy ("AI Plus") treats AI as a practical tool for broad economic integration, not a Manhattan Project for superintelligence. The race framing, he argues, tells us more about who benefits from it in the West than about Beijing's intentions.

Crucially, this isn't complacency. If a genuine race *did* emerge, it would be dangerous — which is why he spends the back half of the conversation on the concrete machinery that could defuse it: track-two dialogues, verification, and governance built into the hardware itself.
