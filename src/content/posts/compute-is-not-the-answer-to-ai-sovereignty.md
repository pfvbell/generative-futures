Every government now wants "AI sovereignty," and most of them think it means buying chips and building data centres. This episode is a sustained argument that they're wrong.

Hamish Low's case is that hardware is the one layer where smaller nations can never win independence — you can spend a fortune on compute and still be a customer of TSMC, ASML, and US export-control policy. Real leverage comes from making yourself *indispensable* instead: dominating the "AI middleware" layer of sector-specific fine-tuning, evaluation, and assurance, where a country like the UK actually has an edge and where things move at software speed.

It's a refreshingly concrete conversation about industrial strategy — including a clear-eyed look at why national-champion bets like Mistral and Cohere mostly disappointed, and what a Chinese-style multi-entrant approach would look like instead.
