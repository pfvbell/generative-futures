This is the founding statement of Generative Futures, the essay that names the problem the whole project is trying to solve.

We are stuck in a debate between two bad options: **inevitability**, the belief that the effects of AI are unavoidable and we can only adapt, and **nostalgia**, the urge to reject new technology outright. Both surrender our agency. The premise of Generative Futures is that there is a third path: technologies are malleable, and their impact is the product of a negotiation we can still influence.

The essay traces how contingent the current path of AI really is, how cheap money, GPUs, and cheap energy shaped which ideas won, and argues that whether AI replaces or empowers people is a political choice, not a technical destiny. If that's right, the most useful thing we can do is start articulating a positive vision now, before the next crisis decides it for us.
