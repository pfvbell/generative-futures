We usually ask what AI will *do*. This conversation asks something stranger and more interesting: how AI is changing our experience of *time*.

Nicklas Berild Lundblad, formerly DeepMind's head of global policy, makes the case that the deep tension of modern life is a mismatch between biological time (our evolved, seasonal, human pace) and computational time (the machine speed our institutions increasingly run on). Social media exploited that gap. But AI, he argues, could instead *mediate* it: an interface that understands human time while operating at machine speed, helping us reclaim agency rather than surrender it.

From there the talk opens out into memory, identity, collective intentionality, and how genuinely alien an AI mind might become. It's one of the most philosophical episodes, and a good answer to anyone who thinks AI can only accelerate the worst of the attention economy.
